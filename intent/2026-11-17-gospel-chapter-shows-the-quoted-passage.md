# INTENT: Gospel chapter shows the quoted passage

## Intent
A new believer, on a Gospel chapter or on the Old Testament chapter it quotes, reads a few English sentences on that page about what the Gospel writer is doing with the passage, and can go to the other chapter.

## Author
Requester, Bible Track — 2026-11-17

## Status
proposed

## Problem
Bible Track records that a chapter was read and never shows that a verse in that chapter is talking to another book. The requester said a verse of a chapter refers to other verses from other books of the Bible, and that this is what a new believer always misses. They said missing these links keeps the believer on the surface. No usage metric exists.

## Proposed outcome
A new believer, on a Gospel chapter or on the Old Testament chapter it quotes, reads a few English sentences on that page about what the Gospel writer is doing with the passage, and can go to the other chapter. Every direct quotation in Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John shows on both chapters. The reader can finish the note without leaving the page. The book names are spelled Isaiah and Philippians, and the chapters on the page use those same spellings.

## Affected users and systems
- New believers reading Bible Track, including the requester (primary)
- The existing chapter checklist, whose visible book names must match the names in the notes
- Allusions, the large cross-reference dump, character family trees, Chinese names, reading plans, a separate study page, a commentary long enough that the reader leaves the chapter, and the rest of the New Testament are out of scope

## Constraints
- English only
- The Gospels first. Direct quotations in Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John. The rest of the New Testament waits
- The note is a few sentences the reader can finish on the chapter
- The same quotation shows on the Gospel chapter and on the Old Testament chapter
- Book names use the corrected English spellings. Isaiah and Philippians. The chapter labels use those same spellings
- The notes appear on the chapter checklist the reader already uses

## Open questions
None
